Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

What If 3D Studio ("the Studio", "we") creates original 3D animated videos with code and, when a creator connects their TikTok account, uploads finished videos to that creator's TikTok inbox as drafts. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

Information we collect

  • TikTok account basics (through TikTok Login Kit, scope user.info.basic): your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar. We use them only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Authorization tokens issued by TikTok (access token and refresh token). They let the Studio upload drafts to your inbox (scope video.upload).

We do not collect your TikTok password, your followers, your messages, your existing videos, analytics, payment information or your contacts.

How we use it

  • To upload videos produced by the Studio to your TikTok inbox as drafts. You review each draft and decide whether to publish it.
  • To show which TikTok account is connected.

We do not publish content directly to your profile, and we do not use your information for advertising.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the Studio's private build environment and are never placed in public code or web pages. Access is limited to the automated process that uploads drafts.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Information is exchanged only with TikTok, as required to upload drafts.

Retention and deletion

We keep tokens only while your account is connected. You can disconnect at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or by contacting us; when you do, we delete the stored tokens.

Children

The Studio is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/efrainiscrying/index.html/issues.
